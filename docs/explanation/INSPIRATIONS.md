# Main Game Loop Draft
Once a user logs in and selects a character, they will be presented with the main interface.
This should look like a cross between Your Chronicle (simple, textual blocky), and the added graphical flair like Magic Research 2.
Skills progress (ala Melvor Idle) - will happen on a separate tab.
At the bottom of the screen (to enhance the visuals), will be an infinite side scrolling battle (like Dwarves: GLory, Death and Loot). The background will reflect the furthest chapter and scene, along with the characters an enemies from that part of the story.
The furthest progress will appear at the top of the list (unlocked, all previous scenes unlocked behind it). Users can click on previous chapters to re-play the story while they level up their character (earning gold for upgrades they can buy from the Melvor Idle skill improvement loop).
In the game, they will have access to any skills (base level from Melvor idle from their highest completion), but will need to unlock and buy gold for them (much like clicker heroes). Every time they re-enter they reset at 0 with 0 skills and nothing unlocked (weak) - but based on their unlocks in the Melvor Idle parts, their base click damage will be gradually higher. As they level their clicker and earn gold (and defeat enough monsters), the monsters will get progressively harder. Minibosses will appear at the end of every chapter section before moving to the next scene.
At the end of the chapter is the major boss (based on the book), and might have different mechanics than just clicking (click in these areas, need to raise melvor idle magic defense or something). Users can die, and they go back to the last completed dot (like clicker heroes) - where they can continue trying to earn gold and leveling up their unlocked skills to possibly advance further.
If they beat the final boss, there is a final cut scene (ending with the ending of the story) - and users can stay in that story zone to continue. From that point forward they comtniue the internal loop (this time with just background music), as enemies keep getting progressively harder and harder (waves of enemies, a minibos) - all of it themed from that chapter. They can choose to stay in the clicking game, or they can go back to the main area. Just like before there will be a set number of enemies to beat (keeps getting more and more as the levels increases), then a miniboss. 
When they exit the story mode (back to YourChronicle interface), they will earn (level balanced) resources based on their progress, level they got to in the story game, and amount of gold they got. They can use these resources to buy resources allowing them to level "train" one Melvor Idle kind of skill (which will be available inside the clicking game).
