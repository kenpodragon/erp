# CHARACTER_GUIDE.md

## Purpose

Development reference for consistent character portrayal across the Elysium Rising mmorPg (ERP). This document is sourced directly from the *Towers of Elysium* book trilogy and serves as the canonical guide for character art direction, dialogue writing, enemy design, and class lore. All game assets depicting named characters or enemy types **must** align with the descriptions in this file.

**Source Material:**
- Book 1: *Elysium Rising* (ER)
- Book 2: *Elysium Fallen* (EF)
- Book 3: *Escape from Elysium* (EFE)

---

## Main Characters

### Protagonists

---

#### 1. Stephen (Aspolin)

- **Full Name / Aliases:** Stephen, Aspolin (divine dreamwalker name), Lord of Wisdom
- **Role:** Primary protagonist. A middle-aged software engineer who discovers he is a divine being incarnated inside Etheris, a cosmic prison realm. He is the anchor-soul placed inside the prison to watch over it and search for his lost children.
- **Age:** 46 in Book 1 (adult timeline); 16 in Book 2 (reset timeline); 46 again in Book 3
- **Physical Description:**
  - Overweight, thinning hair, average height
  - Carries himself with the quiet weight of someone who has lived too many lives
  - In divine form (Aspolin): luminous, radiating authority and ancient grief
  - Martial arts background visible in how he moves under pressure -- trained reflexes beneath a soft exterior
- **Personality:**
  - Deeply analytical (software engineer mind applied to cosmic problems)
  - Self-deprecating humor masking profound pain
  - Fiercely protective of children and loved ones
  - Tendency to intellectualize emotions rather than feel them
  - Stubborn to the point of self-destruction when someone he loves is threatened
  - Carries survivor's guilt across multiple lifetimes
- **Abilities/Powers:**
  - **Un-creation:** Can reverse events, rewind reality itself at enormous personal cost. Used to undo Velistra's death by forcing time backward.
  - **Dreamwalking:** Bridges the conscious and subconscious; carries pieces of dreams into waking reality. Never forgets dreams.
  - **Akashic Access:** Can touch the Akashic Flow -- the living memory of all things
  - **Leaker:** Psychic energy hemorrhages across dimensional boundaries. Unlike normal souls, he is not properly recycled on death.
- **Key Relationships:**
  - **Jamie (Velistra):** Twin flame. Their connection transcends all resets and prison cycles.
  - **Xavier:** Son. An "impossible child" pulled into existence through sheer desperate love.
  - **Whitney/Hannah:** Daughter figure. Dream-walker who breaks through from outside.
  - **Benji:** Guide, ally, sardonic companion.
  - **Lady Illkeserod:** Mentor/commander in the resistance.
  - **The Construct Wife:** His NPC prison guard, deliberately nameless, the antagonist of his domestic prison.
- **First Appearance:** ER Prologue 1 (adult), EF Chapter 1 (teen reset), EFE Chapter 1 (adult loop)
- **Visual Direction:**
  - **Colors:** Deep midnight blue, silver-white accents, muted earth tones
  - **Mood:** Weary dignity. A man who looks ordinary but whose eyes hold something ancient.
  - **Art Style:** Realistic with subtle cosmic distortion -- faint star-field patterns visible in shadows around him. When Aspolin awakens, cracks of white-gold light appear along his skin like a breaking shell.

#### 2. Jamie (Velistra)

- **Full Name / Aliases:** Jamie, Velistra, Velistra Seducera (corrupted form), Angel of Death/Creativity
- **Role:** Stephen's twin flame. Originally a divine being (avatar from another reality), she was shattered by Yaldabaoth, her memories wiped, and her essence corrupted into an archon. Incarnated as Jamie inside the prison.
- **Physical Description:**
  - Dark hair
  - Scent of vanilla and peaches (a recurring sensory anchor across timelines)
  - In teen timeline (EF): fierce, sharp-featured teenage girl with an intensity that frightens people
  - In corrupted archon form: something inhuman moves beneath her skin like living tattoos of writhing darkness
  - In divine form (Velistra): a warrior, radiant and terrible
- **Personality:**
  - Fierce and pragmatic -- starts wars with determination
  - Creative (connects to her domain of Death/Creativity)
  - Protective to the point of violence
  - Carries an edge of danger; people sense something not-quite-human about her even when she appears normal
  - Her love is aggressive, consuming, and transformative
- **Abilities/Powers:**
  - **Domain of Death and Creativity:** The fundamental duality -- destruction that enables new creation
  - **Archon Form:** When corrupted, serves as "The Chain of Desire" -- a weapon of seduction and corruption
  - **Reality Perception:** Can see through the prison's illusions faster than most
  - **Combat:** Inhuman speed and strength; catches thrown punches in iron grips
- **Key Relationships:**
  - **Stephen (Aspolin):** Twin flame. Their bond is the cathode-anode connection holding the prison architecture.
  - **Whitney/Hannah:** Maternal bond
  - **Benji:** Ally
- **First Appearance:** ER (referenced), EF Chapter 3 (teen Jamie), EFE Chapter 5 (adult Jamie)
- **Visual Direction:**
  - **Colors:** Deep crimson and black, vanilla-cream highlights, dark purple shadows
  - **Mood:** Beautiful danger. A blade wrapped in silk.
  - **Art Style:** Contrast between soft human beauty and something sharp and inhuman underneath. In her corrupted form, dark tendrils like living tattoos writhe beneath translucent skin. In divine Velistra form, she blazes with creative fire -- destruction and beauty inseparable.

#### 3. Xavier

- **Full Name / Aliases:** Xavier (no surname given in-text)
- **Role:** Stephen's 14-year-old son. An "impossible child" -- a glitch in the prison system. Stephen's desperate love for a child pulled Xavier's soul across dimensions and forced it into the Construct Wife's flesh, which was never designed to bear children.
- **Physical Description:**
  - Teenage boy, 14 years old
  - Digital native -- always on Discord, gaming
  - Pale when frightened (noted during the Tennessee farm scenes)
  - Injured ankle (EFE Tennessee arc)
- **Personality:**
  - Sarcastic teenager with a sharp tongue
  - Digital native sensibility -- references memes, searches things on his phone mid-crisis
  - Brave despite fear (follows Stephen into supernatural danger)
  - Processing extraordinary revelations with teen coping mechanisms
  - Underneath the sarcasm: deeply loves his father and is terrified of losing him
- **Abilities/Powers:**
  - **Probability Manipulation:** Can alter probability fields. His father's trailer becomes a "probability cloud" in his presence.
  - **Chaos Domain:** Connected to fundamental chaos/randomness in the substrate
  - **System Glitch:** His very existence is an anomaly the prison cannot account for
- **Key Relationships:**
  - **Stephen:** Father. Their bond is powerful enough to tear holes in reality.
  - **The Construct Wife:** Mother figure (NPC), but he is real while she is not
- **First Appearance:** EFE Chapter 1 (heard on Discord), active from EFE Chapter 7 onward
- **Visual Direction:**
  - **Colors:** Electric blue and static-white, glitch-pattern accents
  - **Mood:** Digital chaos incarnate. A teenager who shouldn't exist, crackling with impossible energy.
  - **Art Style:** Subtle digital artifacts around him -- pixels that don't quite resolve, probability waves visible as faint heat-shimmer distortions. Normal-looking kid until you notice the edges of reality bending around him.

#### 4. Whitney / Hannah

- **Full Name / Aliases:** Whitney (chosen name), Hannah (original/reclaimed name), William (dead name, pre-transition)
- **Role:** Stephen and Jamie's divine daughter, reincarnated. Trans woman. Dream-walker who sends a signal from outside the prison that cracks the reset cycle. Her signal is the inciting event of Book 3.
- **Physical Description:**
  - In teen timeline (EF): first year being open about her identity at school; nervous energy when about to say something important
  - Wiccan and occult practitioner -- carries grimoires
  - In the void/purgatory: flailing, weightless, blind -- drifting in infinite dark
- **Personality:**
  - Fierce determination ("the kind that starts wars")
  - Gentle but insistent when asking important questions
  - Deeply spiritual -- turned to Wicca after rejecting the Christian fundamentalism that condemned her
  - Brave enough to risk everything to send a signal through the prison walls
  - Domain of Serenity and Joy -- an anchor of calm in chaos
- **Abilities/Powers:**
  - **Dream-Walking:** Navigates between realities through dream-space
  - **Signal Transmission:** Broke through the prison's barriers with a three-word message
  - **Serenity/Joy Domain:** Stabilizing presence; her voice "cuts through chaos like a scalpel"
- **Key Relationships:**
  - **Stephen:** Father figure. Her signal to him breaks the reset.
  - **Jamie:** She finds Jamie ("I found Jay...")
  - **Patrick:** Best friend. Patrick was the first person she told about her identity. Patrick's family adopted her.
  - **Becca:** Couch-surfs at her place
- **First Appearance:** EF Chapter 4 (teen Whitney), EFE Prologue (voice signal), EFE Chapter 14 (purgatory)
- **Visual Direction:**
  - **Colors:** Soft violet, moonlight silver, warm amber
  - **Mood:** A beacon in darkness. Warmth and defiance.
  - **Art Style:** Surrounded by a faint luminous glow -- her signal still radiating outward. In the void, she is a single point of stubborn light against infinite black. Grimoires and occult symbols orbit her presence.

#### 5. Benji (Benjamin Castellan)

- **Full Name / Aliases:** Benji, Benjamin Castellan, Third Class Dimensional Interlocutor and Temporary Assistant to the Recursive Memory Department
- **Role:** Resistance member operating outside the prison. Originally created as Stephen's guardian angel by Yaldabaoth, he gained free will through a synchronicity between Stephen calling out to Lady Illkeserod and Benji's formation in another timeline. Now works for the resistance.
- **Physical Description (Shapeshifter -- multiple forms):**
  - **Human/Demon Form:** Middle-aged demon in a cardigan (or vest -- "the cardigan's in the wash"). Horns poking through badly thinning hair. Reading glasses held together with electrical tape. Eyes embedded in each skin cell running up his arms.
  - **Horror Form:** Writhing mass of exposed muscle and bone wearing a trench coat made of flayed skin. Moths with human teeth bursting from eye sockets of severed heads crowning his shoulders like grotesque epaulettes.
  - **Goblin Form:** Mostly goblin with a hint of middle management
  - **Winged Eye Form:** A single burning eye wreathed in flame with wings (referenced in EFE)
  - **Chitin Beast Form:** Armored, inhuman combat form
  - **Stabilized Form (EFE resistance scenes):** Cardigan flickering between colors that don't belong to the spectrum. Looks smaller than other gods but older somehow.
- **Personality:**
  - Warm, sarcastic, paternal
  - Bureaucratic humor ("the paperwork is killing me")
  - Offers juice boxes in moments of existential crisis
  - Genuinely cares about Stephen despite cosmic detachment
  - Self-deprecating about his own limitations ("40% helpful and 60% frustrating")
  - "A survivor who knows what eternity costs"
- **Abilities/Powers:**
  - **Shapeshifting:** Shifts between radically different physical forms
  - **Dimensional Navigation:** Can create and stabilize liminal meeting spaces between dimensions
  - **Combat Shifting:** Collapses into "a line of pure intention" -- cuts through space, reforms explosively
  - **Protective Wards:** Creates complex geometric sigils that repel spectres
  - **Portal Maintenance:** Helps hold open escape portals
- **Key Relationships:**
  - **Stephen:** Ward/friend. Was supposed to be his guardian angel.
  - **Lady Illkeserod:** Boss. She rebuilt him after near-destruction.
  - **Whitney:** Ally. Desperate to find her after her signal.
- **First Appearance:** EF Chapter 8 (DMV liminal space), EFE Chapter 13 (resistance council)
- **Visual Direction:**
  - **Colors:** Warm brown cardigan tones flickering to impossible spectrum colors; fire-orange for the winged eye; bone-white and crimson for horror form
  - **Mood:** Cozy horror. A warm cup of tea served by something with too many eyes.
  - **Art Style:** His visual design should constantly shift subtly -- the cardigan is never quite the same color twice. In game idle animations, small horrific details should flicker in and out (an extra eye, a mouth where there shouldn't be one) before snapping back to the friendly middle-manager appearance.

#### 6. Lady Illkeserod

- **Full Name / Aliases:** Lady Illkeserod, Lady I, Ilkse, Goddess of Wisdom/Secrets
- **Role:** Leader of the resistance operating outside Etheris. Two fused souls (twin sisters Selyr and Keserod) who chose to merge into something new -- something vast enough to oversee the Akashic Records and hold the memory of all things.
- **Physical Description:**
  - Body woven from translucent filaments of thought
  - Skin like polished moonstone (when appearing more solid)
  - Eyes holding the depth of collapsed stars
  - Wears something that might be a dress or might be shadows woven into fabric
  - Reality ripples around her like water disturbed by stones
  - Lines of blue-white light run beneath translucent skin -- a lattice of energy threaded through memory
  - Filaments of light flow from her fingers
  - When strained, her projection shatters into a thousand shards of self
- **Personality:**
  - Serene elegance with boundless intellect
  - Otherworldly calm with an edge of gravity
  - Both encouraging and solemn
  - Patient and eternal
  - Carries "the weight of cosmic sadness"
  - Melodic, layered voice (multiple recordings of herself played simultaneously)
- **Abilities/Powers:**
  - **Akashic Flow Manipulation:** Controls/reads the living memory of all existence
  - **Memory Preservation:** Remembers every instant of every memory, back before rebellion was possible
  - **Projection:** Can project her form across dimensional barriers (at great cost)
  - **Reconstruction:** Rebuilt Benji from fragments after he was destroyed
  - **Dreamscape Guidance:** Nurtures dreamwalker potential
- **Key Relationships:**
  - **Stephen (Aspolin):** She trained him as a dreamwalker. Believes he may be their only shot at fixing everything.
  - **Benji:** Her agent/friend. She saved him.
  - **The Thirteen:** Fellow resistance gods
- **First Appearance:** ER (Aspolin training sequences), EF Chapter 8 (referenced), EFE Chapter 5 (physical appearance)
- **Visual Direction:**
  - **Colors:** Blue-white luminescence, moonstone silver, deep void-black
  - **Mood:** Living library. A being made of memory itself.
  - **Art Style:** Translucent and shifting -- her body should look like it's woven from threads of light, each thread containing flowing text or symbols. When she moves, afterimages trail behind containing fragments of memories. Her edges are never sharp -- she bleeds into the space around her.

#### 7. Patrick

- **Full Name / Aliases:** Patrick (surname not specified in text)
- **Role:** Stephen's high school friend. Former Air Force cryptographer who discovered interdimensional communication through DMT experimentation. Lives as a hermit. In the teen timeline, he is the steady calm of the group; in the adult timeline, he is a reclusive figure living in the cracks of the system.
- **Physical Description:**
  - Holds his shoulders like he's perpetually at attention (military bearing)
  - Clove cigarette smoker
  - Unshakable calm that used to annoy Stephen
  - In construct/NPC mode: moves with security-camera precision, head swiveling mechanically
- **Personality:**
  - Quiet, observant, steady
  - Military precision in speech and movement
  - Deeply loyal -- was the first person Whitney told about her identity, and his family adopted her
  - Dark undertones ("Or it escapes," he adds darkly)
  - Processing trauma beneath controlled exterior
- **Abilities/Powers:**
  - **Interdimensional Communication:** Uses DMT-based methods to communicate across dimensional boundaries
  - **Cryptographic Mind:** Air Force training gives him pattern-recognition skills
  - **Coordinates/Data Processing:** Recites precise coordinates and probability assessments
- **Key Relationships:**
  - **Whitney:** Best friend. His family adopted her.
  - **Stephen:** High school friend, fellow traveler
  - **The Group:** Core member of the teen resistance (EF)
- **First Appearance:** EF Chapter 3 (teen timeline), EFE Chapter 20 (adult hermit)
- **Visual Direction:**
  - **Colors:** Military olive, smoke gray, clove-brown
  - **Mood:** Controlled intensity. A cipher wrapped in calm.
  - **Art Style:** Sharp, angular features. Always slightly apart from the group. Clove cigarette smoke should have an otherworldly quality -- forming brief geometric patterns before dissipating.

#### 8. TJ (Thomas James O'Malley)

- **Role:** Excommunicated Catholic priest. Dug too deep into esoteric texts, discovered that the Church is an unknowing front -- a belief engine feeding power to the prison. Runs a halfway house in Orlando helping addicts and runaways. Maintains a basement library of books the Vatican burned centuries ago.
- **Physical Description:**
  - Shoulders seem broader every day (EF teen timeline)
  - Wears ironic t-shirts (Reagan/Clinton '83)
  - Fidgets with clothing
  - In adult timeline: weathered, living rough
- **Personality:**
  - Cannot be bullshitted -- "He's been living in the cracks for years. He'll know if you're lying."
  - Passionate about truth regardless of institutional cost
  - Protective of the vulnerable (runs halfway house)
  - Apostate faith -- lost religion but not spirituality
- **Abilities/Powers:**
  - **Esoteric Knowledge:** Access to forbidden texts describing the prison from the inside
  - **Faith-Based Resistance:** Understanding of how belief engines power the prison
- **Key Relationships:**
  - **Stephen:** High school friend
  - **The Group:** Core member
  - **The Church:** Former priest, now declared heretic
- **First Appearance:** EF Chapter 3 (teen timeline), EFE Chapter 20 (adult, excommunicated)
- **Fate:** Killed by Nyarlathotep's cultists (King in Yellow followers)
- **Visual Direction:**
  - **Colors:** Black clerical remnants, warm amber (halfway house warmth), blood red
  - **Mood:** Fallen saint. Faith broken and rebuilt into something sharper.
  - **Art Style:** Priest's collar visible but torn or modified. Surrounded by the aesthetic of forbidden knowledge -- marginalia, Latin text, sigils scratched into surfaces.

### Antagonists

#### 1. Yaldabaoth / I AM / Sam

- **Full Name / Aliases:** Yaldabaoth, I AM, Sam (original mortal identity), The Demiurge
- **Role:** The primary antagonist. An anti-reality parasite imprisoned inside Etheris. Originally a shepherd boy named Sam who watched his flock die and decided he'd rather be god than victim. His love was corrupted into consuming hunger. The entire prison was built to contain him, but a trillion trillion trillion innocent souls were trapped as collateral damage.
- **Physical Description:**
  - **Mortal Origin:** A shepherd boy (Sam) whose grief and rage became divine
  - **Demiurge Form:** Roiling mass of shadow and mouths, pulsing with greedy anticipation. Unfolds in directions that don't have names, revealing geometries that make eyes water. Wears divinity like an ill-fitting thrift store suit.
  - **Father Mask:** Sometimes wears Stephen's abusive father's face -- red face, bloodshot eyes, leather belt
  - **Final Form:** So vast he blots out the sky
- **Personality:**
  - Megalomaniacal -- prefers the name "I AM"
  - On autopilot for centuries -- like "a CEO who forgot he owns the company"
  - Tediously predictable: consume, expand, collapse, repeat
  - "Terrible management skills"
  - Originally driven by grief-turned-rage, now driven by pure consuming hunger
- **Abilities/Powers:**
  - **Soul Harvesting:** Feeds on souls through cycles of death and rebirth
  - **Reality Architecture:** Controls the prison realm's fundamental structure
  - **Construct Creation:** Creates NPCs (construct wives, diagnostic tools, guardian angels)
  - **Memory Wipe:** Can erase memories across resets
  - **Archon Corruption:** Shattered Velistra and remade her as a weapon
  - **Father-Mask:** Inhabits abusers' forms to torment prisoners
- **Key Relationships:**
  - **Stephen (Aspolin):** Primary threat to his prison
  - **Velistra/Jamie:** Corrupted her into his servant
  - **Metatron:** His perfect enforcer/architect
  - **The Thirteen:** His jailers from outside
  - **Sophia:** Her curiosity inadvertently birthed him (Gnostic origin -- the Archon born from Sophia's error)
- **First Appearance:** ER (referenced), EF Chapter 18 ("The Torturer's Name is I AM"), EFE throughout
- **Visual Direction:**
  - **Colors:** Void-black, sickly yellow-green, bruise-purple, blood-red eyes
  - **Mood:** Cosmic abuse. The weight of being watched by something that feeds on suffering.
  - **Art Style:** Never fully visible. Always partially obscured -- mouths opening in shadow, geometries that hurt to perceive. In his "father" form, hyper-real domestic horror: a perfectly ordinary abusive parent rendered with nauseating clarity. His true form should make the screen distort.

#### 2. The Construct Wife

- **Full Name / Aliases:** Deliberately nameless. "Your NPC wife." "A prison guard wearing a wedding ring."
- **Role:** Stephen's assigned partner inside the prison. An NPC designed to keep him docile, controlled, and spiritually suppressed. Alternates between emotional abuse and calculated affection. Was never designed to bear children (making Xavier's existence impossible).
- **Physical Description:**
  - Deliberately generic -- the books never give her distinguishing physical features. She is designed to be forgettable.
  - In EFE: follows scripted behavior (drives Stephen to Target while humming to pop music, perfectly content and oblivious)
  - Destroys Stephen's computer in a rage
- **Personality:**
  - Oscillates between emotional abuse and scripted affection
  - Scripts activate to achieve goals, then terminate ("the script had achieved its goal and terminated")
  - Comfortable obliviousness -- hums along to pop music moments after a scripted manipulation
  - Not truly sentient -- a construct running programs
- **Abilities/Powers:**
  - **Script Execution:** Runs behavioral programs to manipulate Stephen
  - **Suppression:** Keeps Stephen's spiritual energy contained
  - **Incursion:** In EFE, pursues Stephen through the gateway after he escapes, destabilizing everything
- **First Appearance:** EFE Chapter 1 (domestic scenes), referenced throughout all books
- **Visual Direction:**
  - **Colors:** Washed-out beige, institutional gray, static-white
  - **Mood:** Uncanny valley domesticity. Everything looks normal but feels wrong.
  - **Art Style:** Slightly out of focus. Features that slide off memory. In moments of script activation, her movements become too smooth, too purposeful -- puppet-like precision. When the script ends, she resets like a video game NPC returning to idle.

  - **Mood:** Medical horror. The therapist whose smile has too many teeth.

#### 4. Hastur / The King in Yellow

- **Full Name / Aliases:** Hastur, The King in Yellow, The Unspeakable One
- **Role:** Elder God. Maintains a cult of followers. A being of telepathic madness that mutates its worshippers into beautiful-then-monstrous forms. Neither fully allied with nor opposed to the protagonists -- an obstacle and a gateway.
- **Physical Description:**
  - Impossible head, tilting at impossible angles
  - Projects thoughts directly into minds (words appear as "spikes of ice in the brain")
  - His cultists: start as beautiful humans, mutate into creatures with bone swords and needle teeth
- **Personality:**
  - Alien intelligence
  - Communicates in telepathic ALL-CAPS
  - "THERE IS NO OUT. THERE IS ONLY... DEEPER."
  - Claims to be able to show "everything"
- **Abilities/Powers:**
  - **Telepathy / Madness Induction:** Projects thoughts that feel like physical blows
  - **Mutation:** Transforms followers -- beautiful humans become bone-sword-wielding horrors
  - **Knowledge Brokering:** Claims to know Nyarlathotep, offers to show paths deeper
- **Key Relationships:**
  - **Nyarlathotep:** Fellow elder god, subordinate in hierarchy
  - **His Cult:** Worshippers he transforms
  - **Stephen:** Obstacle/potential dark ally
- **First Appearance:** EFE Chapter 25 (cult encounter)
- **Visual Direction:**
  - **Colors:** Sickly yellow (The Yellow Sign), bone-white, bruised purple
  - **Mood:** Beautiful madness. Seductive wrongness.
  - **Art Style:** Everything around Hastur should look slightly too beautiful -- colors oversaturated, proportions slightly too perfect, until they aren't. His cultists transition from runway-model beauty to bone-and-needle horror. The Yellow Sign should appear as a recurring environmental motif.

#### 5. Nyarlathotep (The Crawling Chaos)

- **Full Name / Aliases:** Nyarlathotep, The Crawling Chaos, The Messenger of the Outer Gods
- **Role:** Trickster elder god. The only elder god who likes to walk among humans. Neither fully ally nor enemy -- he deals in "interesting" rather than right or wrong. Controls access to deeper layers of reality. The key to reaching the Akashic Realm.
- **Physical Description:**
  - A shape that "tilts, as if amused"
  - Wears a suit that changes color -- gray, then canary yellow, then rustic red, always too bright
  - Movements that "tear the fabric of the void"
  - Shadow with too many joints (when in less-human form)
- **Personality:**
  - Amused by everything -- dark, oily amusement
  - Doesn't care about right or wrong; cares about "interesting"
  - Found Yaldabaoth "tediously predictable"
  - Deal-maker and trickster
  - Voice that "slithers" and seems to "smile"
  - The only elder god who could speak to Azathoth and be understood
- **Abilities/Powers:**
  - **Void Manipulation:** Gestures tear the fabric of the void
  - **Signal Control:** Has been muffling Whitney's screams, chooses when to open communications
  - **Gateway Control:** Controls passage to deeper reality layers
  - **Reality Walking:** Can appear anywhere, any time -- just "there" without walking
  - **Knowledge of the Key:** Knows the "name" that serves as the back door to the prison
- **Key Relationships:**
  - **Azathoth:** The blind idiot god -- Nyarlathotep is his messenger
  - **Stephen:** Reluctant informant/deal-maker
  - **Whitney:** Has been muffling her signals
  - **Yaldabaoth:** Finds him boring
- **First Appearance:** EFE Chapter 27 (first contact in void), EFE Chapter 34+ (gateway scenes)
- **Visual Direction:**
  - **Colors:** Shifting -- canary yellow, rustic red, void-black, oil-slick iridescence
  - **Mood:** Amused malevolence. A used-car salesman selling cosmic secrets.
  - **Art Style:** His suit should never be the same color in consecutive frames. Shadow-play around him -- too many limbs visible in peripheral vision, perfectly normal when looked at directly. Movements should have an unsettling fluidity, like joints that bend in extra directions.

#### 6. Todd

- **Full Name / Aliases:** Todd (surname not specified)
- **Role:** Leader of the Red Hat rebellion. A conspiracy theorist from the bottom of the socioeconomic ladder who becomes the figurehead of an organic/anti-technology movement that wages holy war against S Corp. Ironically, his rebellion serves Yaldabaoth's interests by attacking the very innovations that threaten the prison.
- **Physical Description:**
  - Unshaven, stubble verging on a beard

## Player Character Classes

These are the four playable classes in the game. Each draws from the trilogy's mythology.

| Old Name | New Name | Theme | Primary Stat | Accent Color |
|----------|----------|-------|-------------|-------------|
| Sentinel | **Engineer** | Eternal Engine tech, construction/defense | STR | `#c41e3a` (crimson) |
| Arcanist | **Conduit** | Cosmic energy channeling, reality manipulation | INT | `#7b5ea7` (purple) |
| Wanderer | **Drifter** | Memory-walker, navigates between realities | AGI | `#00d4ff` (cyan) |
| Invoker | **Vessel** | Channels cosmic entities, system manipulation | INT/STR | `#d4af37` (gold) |

---

### Engineer (formerly Sentinel)

- **Lore Blurb:** Those who hear the Eternal Engine's rhythm and bend its mechanisms to their will. Engineers rebuild what the prison unmakes, constructing shields and weapons from the substrate's own architecture. Where Yaldabaoth tears reality apart, Engineers hammer it back together -- stronger than before.
- **Visual Direction:** Heavy armor built from repurposed prison-substrate materials. Crimson energy lines tracing circuit-like patterns across plating. Tools that are part weapon, part construction -- hammers that reshape reality, shields that solidify from ambient energy. Aesthetic is industrial-divine: factory floor meets cathedral.
- **Gameplay Focus:** Tank/builder. High durability, construct-based abilities, area denial, team shielding.

---

### Conduit (formerly Arcanist)

- **Lore Blurb:** Conduits tap into the raw cosmic energy that bleeds through the prison's cracks -- the same Akashic Flow that Lady Illkeserod wields. They are living channels for forces too vast to comprehend, burning bright and burning fast. Every spell is a conversation with infinity, and infinity does not always answer kindly.
- **Visual Direction:** Flowing robes or light armor with translucent, filament-like energy running through the fabric (inspired by Lady Illkeserod's body). Hands glow with Akashic blue-white. Eyes occasionally show depth-of-collapsed-stars effect. Sigils and geometric patterns orbit them during spellcasting. Aesthetic is cosmic-scholar: Akashic library meets reality breach.
- **Gameplay Focus:** Ranged DPS / support. Reality manipulation, energy projection, buff/debuff through Akashic knowledge.

---

### Drifter (formerly Wanderer)

- **Lore Blurb:** Drifters walk the spaces between -- the liminal nowhere that Benji calls home, the purgatory where Whitney screams into the void, the dream-paths Aspolin once traveled. They remember what the prison forces everyone else to forget. Every step carries them closer to a reality that might not exist yet, or one that stopped existing ages ago.
- **Visual Direction:** Light, fluid clothing that seems slightly out of phase -- edges that blur and shift. Cyan energy trails behind movement like afterimages. Equipment has a dreamlike quality: weapons that look partially translucent, armor that ripples like water. Aesthetic is liminal-traveler: DMV-between-dimensions meets dream-corridor.
- **Gameplay Focus:** High mobility / evasion. Phase-shifting, memory attacks, dimensional dodge, scouting.

---

### Vessel (formerly Invoker)

- **Lore Blurb:** Vessels open themselves to the cosmic entities that hunger at reality's edges -- Tiamat's serpentine power, Hecate's triple sight, even fragments of the Crawling Chaos himself. They are the willing hosts, the brave or foolish souls who let something vast pour through their mortal frame. The power is immense. The cost is written on their changing flesh.
- **Visual Direction:** Their appearance shifts based on which entity they are channeling. Base form shows subtle marks of channeling: eyes that occasionally split into three (Hecate), scales that shimmer on skin (Tiamat), shadows with too many joints (Nyarlathotep). Gold energy as the binding force holding them together. Aesthetic is divine-possession: human vessel cracking with borrowed godhood.
- **Gameplay Focus:** Hybrid DPS / support. Transformation-based abilities, entity channeling with risk/reward mechanics, high burst power with instability costs.

## Enemy Types

Organized by threat tier, mapping to game enemy classes. All enemies are drawn from beings and creatures that appear in the Towers of Elysium trilogy.

---

### Tier E-D (Common Enemies)

---

#### Pointers / Rogue NPCs
- **Description:** The Pointers are silent figures who appear in crowds, pointing skyward. When the prison destabilizes, some NPCs (constructs designed to maintain the illusion of normality) go rogue -- their scripts corrupted, their behavior erratic. They stand in plazas and beaches, vacant stares, unmoving limbs, sometimes turning violent.
- **Visual Direction:** Uncanny valley humans. Slightly wrong proportions, too-smooth skin, movements that loop or stutter. Eyes that track the player with security-camera precision.
- **Source:** ER Prologue 1 (Pointers), throughout all books (rogue constructs)

#### Fire Beasts
- **Description:** Solidified flame in canine-like form. White-hot eyes. Their howl starts as hunger and ends as broken mathematics. They hunt in packs through corrupted zones.
- **Visual Direction:** Molten, canine silhouettes. Core of white-hot energy, outer shell of solidified flame that cracks and reforms. Eyes are the brightest point -- pure white. Trail of scorched reality behind them.
- **Source:** EFE Chapter 16 (Whitney's purgatory chase)

#### Mi-Go Remnants
- **Description:** Fungoid creatures from Yuggoth. Crab-like chitinous shells split open, exposing pink-gray flesh. Something pulses inside -- not a heart, but a cluster of organs with no human names. A sac of fluid or something else entirely. Their corpses litter battlefields.
- **Visual Direction:** Crustacean-fungal hybrid. Chitinous shells with vertical splits revealing biological horror. Pink-gray organic interiors pulsing with alien life. Colors: shell-brown, fungi-gray, organic pink.
- **Source:** EFE Chapter 33 (Mi-Go battlefield)

---

### Tier C-B (Uncommon Enemies)

---

#### Memory-Eating Spectres
- **Description:** Void-mouthed entities that dissolve into shadows between buildings. They consume memories, leaving victims hollow. Their mouths work silently -- hungry but uncertain. Repelled by complex geometric sigils.
- **Visual Direction:** Semi-transparent dark forms with gaping void-mouths. No features except the mouth -- everything else is absence. They flow like smoke through urban environments. When they feed, the area around the victim desaturates and loses detail.
- **Source:** EFE Chapter 30 (Benji fights spectres in cityscape)

#### Anti-Life Scorpion Constructs
- **Description:** Hulking entities shaped vaguely like scorpions, made entirely of jagged obsidian and spite. Tails snap forward with lethal speed. Diamond-hard carapace. Hunt in groups of three or more.
- **Visual Direction:** Black obsidian exoskeletons with edges that look fractured and sharp. No biological features -- purely construct. Jagged, angular, aggressive geometry. Tail strikes leave cracks in reality itself.
- **Source:** EFE Chapter 19 (attack on Benji and Whitney)

#### King in Yellow Cultists
- **Description:** Start as beautiful humans -- runway-model perfection. Then they mutate. Bone swords erupt from forearms, teeth sharpen to needles, beauty curdles into horror. "Perfect smile widened" into something impossible.
- **Visual Direction:** Phase 1: Impossibly beautiful humans, all black clothing, silver jewelry, pentagram necklaces. Phase 2: Mutation -- bone protrusions, elongating limbs, needle teeth, beauty becoming grotesque. The transition should be disturbing: too-perfect becoming too-wrong. Colors: pre-mutation black/silver, post-mutation bone-white and blood-red, Yellow Sign branded on flesh.
- **Source:** EFE Chapter 25 (occult bookshop encounter, cult ceremony)

---

### Tier A (Rare Enemies)

---

#### Archons (Corrupted Divine Servants)
- **Description:** What happens when Yaldabaoth captures a divine being and twists them. Jamie/Velistra was turned into an archon. These are corrupted entities wearing human faces, darkness moving beneath skin like living tattoos. Backs arch, smiles have too many teeth. They serve as Yaldabaoth's elite enforcers inside the prison.
- **Visual Direction:** Beautiful humanoid forms with visible corruption -- dark tendrils writhing beneath translucent skin, shadows that pour through eyes and mouths. When fully revealed, they are beings of pure corrupted purpose. Colors: shadow-black corruption over whatever their original form's colors were. Living tattoo patterns.
- **Source:** EF Chapter 9 (Jamie's possession), EFE Chapter 5 (archon description)

#### Construct Diagnosticians (Dr. Mellor Type)
- **Description:** Prison diagnostic tools wearing human professional facades -- doctors, therapists, bureaucrats. They run "diagnostics on consensual reality matrices" and file reports in triplicate. When their true nature is triggered, the clinical facade splits to reveal something monstrous underneath.
- **Visual Direction:** Phase 1: Clinical perfection -- white coats, clipboards, professional calm. Phase 2: The clinical tools become weapons, the white coat splits, the professional smile reveals too many teeth. Sterile horror aesthetic.
- **Source:** ER, EF (Dr. Christine Mellor scenes)

#### The Construct Entities (Prison Guards)
- **Description:** High-level prison maintenance constructs wearing human faces. More sophisticated than common NPCs. The Construct Wife is one of these. They alternate between scripted emotional manipulation and cold mechanical purpose. When their cover is blown, movements become puppet-like, too smooth, too purposeful.
- **Visual Direction:** Hyper-normal humans with uncanny valley tells -- movements too precise, emotional transitions too clean, idle animations that loop. When exposed, visible puppet-strings of golden energy connect them upward to an unseen controller.
- **Source:** Throughout all books (Construct Wife, Patrick in NPC mode)

---

### Tier S (Boss Enemies)

---

#### Hastur / The King in Yellow
- **Boss Fight Theme:** Madness and mutation
- **Description:** Elder God encounter. Telepathic attacks that feel like ice spikes in the brain. Transforms the arena -- reality becomes "deeper." Summons mutating cultists as adds. The Yellow Sign spreads across the environment.
- **Visual Direction:** Impossible geometry. A presence more than a form. Sickly yellow light that makes everything look diseased. The arena itself mutates during the fight. Players near him begin to see false images.
- **Mechanics Direction:** Sanity mechanic, environmental transformation, add management (cultists mutating in phases)
- **Source:** EFE Chapter 25-26

#### Nyarlathotep (The Crawling Chaos)
- **Boss Fight Theme:** Deals and trickery
- **Description:** Not a traditional boss fight -- more of a puzzle/negotiation encounter that can turn violent. Appears in a color-shifting suit. His attacks tear the void. He is amused by the attempt to fight him.
- **Visual Direction:** The suit. Always the suit. But the color shifts constantly. Shadows around him have too many limbs. His "attacks" look like casual gestures that happen to rip reality. The arena is the void itself.
- **Mechanics Direction:** Puzzle/deal mechanics mixed with combat. Players must "interest" him to progress. Brute force alone fails.
- **Source:** EFE Chapters 27, 34+

#### Metatron
- **Boss Fight Theme:** Divine machinery and overwhelming force
- **Description:** Yaldabaoth's enforcer leading angelic armies. Wheels within wheels, eyes that never blink. Commands waves of hostile angelic light. Raises his hand and reality responds like a machine receiving input.
- **Visual Direction:** Biblically accurate angel rendered as military-industrial complex. Wheels spinning, eyes tracking, geometric precision. Angelic armies as "a million points of hostile light." The arena is a divine factory floor.
- **Mechanics Direction:** Army-management fight. Players must survive waves of angelic adds while damaging Metatron during vulnerability windows when his wheels desynchronize.
- **Source:** EFE Chapters 50+ (war flashbacks)

---

### Tier SS (World Boss / Raid)

---

#### Yaldabaoth / I AM (Reduced Form)
- **Raid Theme:** The abuser revealed
- **Description:** A reduced manifestation of the Demiurge. Not his full power (that would be the prison itself), but enough to blot out the sky. Roiling mass of shadow and mouths. Unfolds in directions without names. Geometries that cause visual distortion.
- **Visual Direction:** The screen should distort. Shadow and mouths and impossible geometry. Players should feel small. His presence causes the UI itself to glitch subtly. The Father-Mask phases appear as intermission mechanics -- he wears the face of something domestic and awful before returning to cosmic horror.
- **Mechanics Direction:** Multi-phase raid. Phase 1: Father-Mask (domestic horror, psychological attacks). Phase 2: Demiurge reveal (cosmic horror, reality-warping arena). Phase 3: Consuming form (DPS race as he attempts to consume the raid).
- **Source:** EF Chapter 18, EFE final chapters

#### Full Prison Manifestation (Etheris Itself)
- **Raid Theme:** Escape from reality
- **Description:** The ultimate encounter. The prison itself becomes the enemy. Not a creature to fight but a reality to escape. Every system, every NPC, every construct turns hostile. The substrate itself fights back.
- **Visual Direction:** The entire game world warps. Sky cracks. NPCs freeze mid-action, then attack. Buildings reshape into mazes. The prison is not a monster -- it is the world turning against you.
- **Mechanics Direction:** Environmental raid. Players must reach and activate specific points while the world itself opposes them. All previous enemy types appear. The "boss health bar" is the prison's integrity -- players are breaking it from inside.
- **Source:** Conceptual -- derived from the trilogy's core premise of escaping the prison realm
