# REC 2.1: Loop A - Overworld / Hub (Strategy & Navigation)

This document defines the interface and mechanics for the central strategy and navigation hub of the Elysium Rising MMORPG.

---

## 1. Inspirations & Design Baseline
The Overworld Hub is modeled after the following titles. Use them as a reference for UI behavior and gameplay flow:

- **[Your Chronicle](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1546320/Your_Chronicle/):** Primary inspiration for the node-based progression map, chapter-scene hierarchy, and 100% completion/mastery logic.
- **[Magic Research 2](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2864890/Magic_Research_2/):** Baseline for the clean, high-contrast dark aesthetic and efficient menu navigation.
- **[Dwarves: Glory, Death and Loot](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2205850/Dwarves_Glory_Death_and_Loot/):** Visual reference for the side-scrolling pixel art battle banner and equipment-based visual growth.

**Note:** These inspirations provide the foundation. The specific requirements listed below supercede the inspirations where they diverge (e.g., narrative gating and persistent essence).

---

## 2. Hub Interface Layout
Inspired by *Your Chronicle*'s node-based progression and *Magic Research 2*'s minimalist aesthetic.

### 1.1 Layout Components
- **Top Bar:** Persistent information (Avatar, Level, Gold, Essence, Shards).
- **Left Sidebar (Centralized Interface):** Access point for:
  - **Map (Default):** The node-based progression map.
  - **Skills:** Idle Training interface.
  - **Home Base Hub:** Display collections, leaderboard standings, and a personal journal of uncovered story beats.
  - **Shop:** Overworld equipment and resource trading.
  - **Chat:** Social communication (Global and Chapter-specific).
  - **Board:** Community/event notifications.
- **Main Content Area:** Node-based chapter/scene map.
- **Bottom Banner:** Animated side-scrolling pixel art battle scene.

---

## 2. Chapter/Scene Node Map
Interactive map showing chapter and scene progression.

### 2.1 Node States
- `locked`: Greyed out, not yet accessible.
- `available`: Highlighted border, ready to play.
- `in_progress`: Pulsing/glowing indicator, partially completed.
- `completed`: Checkmark, fully cleared.
- `mastered`: Gold border, 100% completion (all branches).

### 2.2 Navigation Logic
- **Chapter-Based Progression:** Divided into levels matching chapters from the *Towers of Elysium* trilogy.
- **Vertical Scroll:** Chapters are listed vertically.
- **Horizontal Flow:** Scenes within a chapter are arranged horizontally.
- **Branching:** Some scenes branch into optional side content or alternate paths.

---

## 3. Chapter Info Panel (Modal)
Clicking a node opens a panel with:
- **Scene Title:** e.g., "1-1: The Gate".
- **Lore Summary:** Brief narrative hook.
- **Stats:** Best score, completion %, and estimated audiobook duration.
- **Action:** "Enter Story Mode" button.

---

## 4. Atmospheric Battle Banner (Visual Feedback)
- **Infinite Side-Scroll:** Continuous animation showing the player character walking/fighting, implemented via **PixiJS** for high-performance WebGL rendering.
- **Framework:** Uses `@pixi/react` to bridge the PixiJS canvas with the React-based Overworld Hub.
- **Scene-Reactive:** Background and enemies change based on the furthest unlocked chapter.
- **Growth Indicator:** Provides visual feedback of character growth (better armor, larger weapons, spell effects) as the player's stats/skills improve.
- **Visibility:** Only visible in the "Map" (Overworld Hub) view.

---

## 5. Home Base Hub
A dedicated section for meta-progression and narrative tracking:
- **Collections:** Display rare items and artifacts found during the journey.
- **Personal Journal:** A collection of uncovered story beats and lore summaries from completed chapters.
- **Leaderboard Standings:** Quick view of the player's rank in global and chapter-specific rankings.

---

## 6. UI/UX Rules
- **Pausing:** Switching from Map to Skills or Shop pauses any active Story Mode session.
- **Dark Theme:** High-contrast text on deep charcoal/black backgrounds.
- **Responsiveness:** Sidebar collapses on mobile; Node Map remains horizontally scrollable.
