# REC 2.2: Loop B - Story Mode / Clicker Combat (Active Play)

This document defines the mechanics for the active gameplay loop, where players progress through the narrative via audiobook-gated combat.

---

## 1. Inspirations & Design Baseline
Story Mode's combat and progression are modeled after the following title. Use it as a baseline for interaction and balance:

- **[Clicker Heroes](https://store.steampowered.com/app/363970/Clicker_Heroes/):** Primary inspiration for the enemy-centric combat, wave progression (1-10), session-based gold upgrades, and "farmable" checkpoints on boss failure.

**Note:** While *Clicker Heroes* provides the mechanical base, the requirements listed below supercede the inspiration where they diverge (e.g., narrative gating, synced text overlays, and server-side validation).

---

## 2. Combat Engine (Clicker Heroes Style)

### 1.1 Core Engine
- **Framework:** Implemented using **PixiJS** via `@pixi/react` for high-performance sprite animation and particle effects (floating numbers).
- **Active Clicking:** Each click deals damage based on character stats and session upgrades.
- **Auto-DPS:** Passive damage based on Idle Training skills.
- **Visuals:** Enemy-centric combat with prominent HP bars and animated sprites.
- **Animations & VFX:**
  - Idle, damage, and death animations for all entities.
  - Specific spell effects for player abilities unlocked via Idle Training.
- **Floating Damage Numbers:** Visual feedback on every click (size/color change for critical hits).

### 1.2 Wave & Boss System
- **Standard Waves:** 1-9 are minions; progressing through 10 enemies per wave.
- **Mini-Boss (Scene Boss):** Appears at Wave 10. Features:
  - Higher HP pool with visible phase transitions.
  - 30-second enrage timer.
  - Unique lore-based abilities from the book.
- **Chapter Boss:** Major multi-phase encounters at the final scene of each chapter. Features:
  - **Target Zones:** Requires clicking specific highlighted areas on the sprite.
  - **Skill Checks:** Minimum Idle Training levels required to survive certain attacks.
- **Failure:** Death resets the session to Wave 9, allowing the player to farm gold for more upgrades.

---

## 2. Narrative Experience & Listening (Eleven Reader)

### 2.1 The Reading Experience
- **Eleven Reader Integration:** Real-time streaming of chapter/scene audio.
- **Synced Text Overlay:** Story text scrolls and appears paragraph-by-paragraph in real-time, synced with the audio.
- **Cinematics:** Chapter-ending cutscenes (post-boss victory) featuring high-fidelity text overlays and atmospheric visuals from the book's climax.
- **Copy Protection:** Story text rendered as images (Kindle-style) to prevent unauthorized extraction.

### 2.2 Strict Narrative Gating
- **Audio-Linked Progression:** Players **cannot fight the boss** or advance to the next node until the corresponding audiobook segment has finished playing.
- **Timer-Based Gate:** Strictly tied to the **1x playback duration**. Even if audio is muted, the player must wait for the segment duration to pass on the first playthrough.
- **Replay Flexibility:** Once completed at 1x speed, subsequent plays allow for faster progression and optional audio.

---

## 3. AI-Driven Visuals & Asset Caching
- **Generation:** Character and enemy designs are derived from narrative descriptions in the *Towers of Elysium* book series.
- **Consistency:** Assets are stored in a globally shared cache to ensure visual consistency and minimize API/generation costs.

---

## 4. In-Session Progression (Temporary)
Every Story Mode session starts fresh to encourage tactical spending.

- **Session Gold:** Earned from enemy kills; resets to 0 on exit.
- **Hero Upgrades:**
  - Increase Click Damage (1.15x scaling cost).
  - Increase Auto-DPS (1.15x scaling cost).
  - Unlock active skills (Fixed costs).

---

## 5. Victory & Meta-Rewards
- **Exit Rewards:** Session performance (waves, gold, bosses) is converted into permanent **Elysium Essence** and Resources.
- **First-Time Bonus:** Significant reward for first clear of a scene.
- **Mastery:** Completion percentage for clearing all branches in a chapter.
- **Progress Tracking:** Chapter XP tracks the player's overall journey through the Tower's narrative.
