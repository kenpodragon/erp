# REC 2.5: Audio & Music Integration

This document defines the technical integration for background music (SUNO), tactile sound effects, and future high-fidelity narrative experiences (Eleven Reader) for the Elysium Rising MMORPG.

---

## 1. Inspirations & Design Baseline
While the audio integration is unique to the *Towers of Elysium* IP, the following baseline applies to atmospheric audio and SFX:

- **[Magic Research 2](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2864890/Magic_Research_2/):** Reference for minimalist but effective audio cues and atmospheric background music that doesn't distract from the core loop.

---

## 2. Audio Player & Content Delivery

### 2.1 Standard Audio Player Embed
- **Functional Requirements:**
    - **Controls:** Play, Pause, Stop/Restart, and Volume.
    - **Playback Speed:** Support for 0.5x, 1.0x, 1.5x, and 2.0x speeds.
    - **Progress Bar:** Interactive seek bar for skipping within a scene (only if previously cleared).
    - **Visualizer:** (Optional) Basic waveform or frequency bars for aesthetic engagement.
- **Bi-directional Sync:**
    - Pausing the audio player MUST pause the combat engine (clicks and Auto-DPS).
    - Pausing the game (via menu) MUST pause the audio player.

### 2.2 Dynamic Music (SUNO)
- **Mood-Based Generation:** SUNO-generated tracks matching the narrative mood of each chapter.
- **Thematic Rotation:** Playlists rotate based on the active chapter (Ambient vs. Battle).
- **Fade Logic:** Implement 2s crossfades between tracks when transitioning scenes.

---

## 3. Tactile Sound Effects (SFX)
Providing tactile feedback for core actions:
- **Combat SFX:** Distinct sounds for clicks, critical hits, and enemy defeats.
- **UI SFX:** Subtle clicks for navigation and celebratory chimes for level-ups/achievements.
- **Boss SFX:** Dramatic entrance sting and phase-shift audio cues.

---

## 4. Advanced Narrative Integration (Future Phase)

This section covers the transition from delay-based text to high-fidelity audiobook narration.

### 4.1 Eleven Reader Integration
- **Real-Time Streaming:** Chapters/scenes correspond to the active story node.
- **Narrative Gating:** 
  - **Playback Tracking:** Server-side tracking of audio progress is mandatory to enforce gates.
  - **Replay Flexibility:** After completing a segment once at 1x speed, subsequent playthroughs allow for faster progression and optional audio.

### 4.2 Synced Text & Audio
- **Synced Text Overlay:** Story text scrolls and appears paragraph-by-paragraph in real-time, synced with audio playback position.
- **Sync Mapping:** Retrieve timestamp-to-asset mapping from the DB (`scene_audio_sync` table).
- **Precision:** Use ElevenLabs word-level timestamps (alignment) for perfect synchronization.
- **Copy Protection:** Story text rendered as images (Kindle-style) to prevent unauthorized extraction.

---

## 5. Technical Constraints
- **1x Speed Gate:** Validation on the first playthrough is strictly tied to the narrative's original duration (audio or calculated).
- **Caching:** Globally shared cache for audio assets to ensure performance and reduce generation cost.
