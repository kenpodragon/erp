# REC 2.5: Audio & Music Integration

This document defines the technical integration for audiobook narration (Eleven Reader) and dynamic music (SUNO) for the Elysium Rising MMORPG.

---

## 1. Inspirations & Design Baseline
While the audio integration is unique to the *Towers of Elysium* IP, the following baseline applies to atmospheric audio and SFX:

- **[Magic Research 2](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2864890/Magic_Research_2/):** Reference for minimalist but effective audio cues and atmospheric background music that doesn't distract from the core loop.

**Note:** The requirements listed below for **Narrative Gating** and **Synced Text Overlay** are unique to ERP and supercede standard idle game audio implementations.

---

## 2. Audiobook Narration (Eleven Reader)

### 1.1 Content Delivery & Sync
- **Real-Time Streaming:** Chapters/scenes correspond to the active story node.
- **Narrative Gating:** 
  - **Playback Tracking:** Server-side tracking of audio progress is mandatory to enforce gates.
  - **Replay Flexibility:** After completing a segment once at 1x speed, subsequent playthroughs allow for faster progression and optional audio.

### 1.2 The Reading Experience
- **Synced Text Overlay:** Story text scrolls and appears paragraph-by-paragraph in real-time, synced with audio playback position.
- **Copy Protection:** Story text rendered as images (Kindle-style) to prevent unauthorized extraction.

---

## 2. Dynamic Music (SUNO)

### 2.1 Thematic Playlists
- **Mood-Based Generation:** SUNO-generated tracks that match the narrative mood/atmosphere of each chapter.
- **Thematic Rotation:** Playlists rotate based on the chapter's thematic progression.
- **Separate Volume:** Independent sliders for Narration, Music, and SFX.

### 2.2 Scene Transitions
- **Atmosphere Shift:** Crossfades between tracks when moving between scenes or entering boss encounters.

---

## 3. Tactile Sound Effects (SFX)
Providing tactile feedback for core actions:
- **Combat SFX:** Distinct sounds for clicks, critical hits, and enemy defeats.
- **UI SFX:** Subtle clicks for navigation and celebratory chimes for level-ups/achievements.
- **Boss SFX:** Dramatic entrance sting and phase-shift audio cues.

---

## 4. Technical Constraints
- **1x Speed Gate:** Validation on the first playthrough is strictly tied to the audio's original duration.
- **Caching:** Globally shared cache for audio assets to ensure performance and reduce generation cost.
