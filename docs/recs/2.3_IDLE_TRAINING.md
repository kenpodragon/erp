# REC 2.3: Loop C - Idle Training (Passive Progression)

This document defines the mechanics for the passive skill training system, which provides the foundational stats for Story Mode.

---

## 1. Inspirations & Design Baseline
Idle Training is modeled after the following title. Use it as a baseline for skill depth and progression feel:

- **[Melvor Idle](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1267910/Melvor_Idle/):** Primary inspiration for the "one-at-a-time" training system, skill variety, long-term progression, and the 1-99 leveling curve.

**Note:** While *Melvor Idle* provides the mechanical base, the requirements listed below supercede the inspiration where they diverge (e.g., active play boosts, integration with Story Mode stats, and Elysium Essence consumption).

---

## 2. Skill-Based Growth (Melvor Idle Style)

### 1.1 One-at-a-Time Training
- Players select a single skill to train (Combat, Magic, or Utility).
- Switching skills stops the previous one immediately.
- XP is granted every tick (e.g., every 2 seconds).
- Training rate is modeled after *Melvor Idle*.

### 1.2 XP & Leveling
- XP Curve: `XP_for_Level(N) = Floor(N + 300 * 2^(N/7)) / 4`.
- Max Level: 99.
- Mastery: Additional levels providing diminishing stat bonuses.

---

## 2. Skill Categories (Rule of 4)
Initial implementation focuses on 4 primary skills:
- **Attack:** Increases base Click Damage in Story Mode.
- **Magic:** Increases base Auto-DPS and unlocks in-session magic skills.
- **Lore:** Reduces story duration gate (minor %) and increases Essence gain.
- **Precision:** Increases critical hit chance and crit multiplier.

---

## 3. Background Execution
- **Persistence:** Training continues while the player is offline and while playing Story Mode.
- **Offline Cap:** Maximum of 12 hours of offline progress initially.
- **Essence Consumption:** Training consumes permanent **Elysium Essence** over time.

---

## 4. Manual Advancement (Active Play Boost)
Users can actively boost their training progress:
- **Manual Clicker Mode:** Users can click into a specific skill to play a mini-game version of the clicker mode.
- **Enemy Pool:** Enemies encountered in manual mode come from the pool of any enemies the player has unlocked up to that point in the books.
- **Percentage Boost:** Gold and kills earned during these manual sessions provide a significant percentage boost to the remaining training time for that skill level.
- **Example:** If the idle level-up time is 5 hours, clearing level 500 in manual mode might grant a 100% boost to the remaining time.

---

## 5. Session Floor (Permanent Progression)
- **Base Stats:** Idle Training levels determine the "base stats" and power floor for all Story Mode sessions.
- **Character XP:** Permanent growth via Idle Training contributes to overall character level and stat benchmarks.
- **Example:** `Base_Click_Damage = 1 + (Attack_Level / 2)`.
- This ensures that long-term idle progression makes active play progressively more efficient.
