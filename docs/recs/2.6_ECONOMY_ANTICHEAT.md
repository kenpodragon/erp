# REC 2.6: Economy & Anti-Cheat

This document defines the dual-economy system and the server-authoritative validation mechanisms for the Elysium Rising MMORPG.

---

## 1. Inspirations & Design Baseline
The dual-economy and validation systems are modeled after the following titles:

- **[Clicker Heroes](https://store.steampowered.com/app/363970/Clicker_Heroes/):** Baseline for the session-reset mechanic and gold-based temporary upgrades.
- **[Melvor Idle](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1267910/Melvor_Idle/):** Baseline for the permanent resource economy and long-term meta-progression.
- **[Magic Research 2](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2864890/Magic_Research_2/):** Reference for the visual polish and tactile feel of a high-fidelity incremental economy.

**Note:** These titles provide the foundation. The requirements listed below supercede the inspirations where they diverge (e.g., Elysium Essence consumption rates and anti-cheat validation rules).

---

## 2. Dual Economy System

### 1.1 Persistent Resources (Meta-Progression)
- **Elysium Essence:** Permanent currency earned via Story Mode completions and specific achievements.
- **Usage:** Consumed by Idle Training, used for meta-upgrades and equipment.
- **Persistence:** Stored in the database per user.

### 1.2 Temporary Gold (In-Session)
- **Session Gold:** Earned via enemy kills in Story Mode.
- **Usage:** In-session upgrades (Click Damage, Auto-DPS, Session Skills).
- **Persistence:** Resets to 0 upon exiting Story Mode.

### 1.3 Premium Currency (Elysium Shards)
- **Purchased:** Via Stripe integration.
- **Usage:** Cosmetics, auto-train tokens, and quality-of-life (QoL) boosters.

---

## 2. Advanced Anti-Cheat & Validation

### 2.1 Server-Authoritative Logic
- **Real-Time Validation:** Server calculates "true" damage and gold drops.
- **Click Validation:**
  - Rate Limiting: Flag click rates > 15 CPS.
  - Damage Validation: Re-verify all reported damage against permanent character stats.
- **Playback-Based Gating:** Server-side validation of audio progress is required to unlock scene completion.

### 2.2 Behavioral Detection
- **Human vs. Bot:** System analyzes click timing variability and mouse movement paths to distinguish between human players and automated macros/bots.
- **Pattern Matching:** Repeatedly uniform click intervals (e.g., exactly 100ms between clicks) are automatically flagged.

### 2.3 Reward Integrity
- **Final Session Validation:** On Story Mode exit, the backend re-calculates all session gains before granting permanent Elysium Essence to prevent client-side injection.

---

## 3. Aesthetic Polish (Magic Research 2 Style)
The entire economic and anti-cheat layer is wrapped in a high-fidelity, polished UI:
- **Responsive Feedback:** Instant, optimistic UI for clicks, with server-synced corrections.
- **Dark Aesthetic:** Deep charcoal backgrounds with high-contrast text and glowing status indicators.
- **Micro-animations:** Subtle numeric increments and resource flow animations to make the economy feel tactile and "alive."
