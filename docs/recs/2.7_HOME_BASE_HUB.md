# REC 2.7: Loop A - Home Base Hub (Meta-Progression & Narrative)

This document defines the high-fidelity meta-progression dashboard for the Elysium Rising MMORPG.

---

## 1. Overview & Aesthetics
The **Home Base Hub** is the player's sanctum within the Tower. It serves as the primary interface for reviewing narrative progress, managing long-term collectibles, and tracking global status.

### 1.1 Aesthetic Style: *The Terminal*
- **Visuals:** High-contrast monospaced typography (e.g., Fira Code, JetBrains Mono).
- **Color Palette:** Deep black backgrounds with amber (#ffb000) or matrix-green (#00ff41) accents.
- **Interactions:** Subtle CRT scanline overlays, flickering text effects on load, and "mechanical" click sound effects.
- **Layout:** A long-scrolling vertical layout divided into distinct "Terminals" or "Modules."

---

## 2. Terminal 1: The Akashic Log (Personal Journal)
A record of every story beat the player has encountered.

### 2.1 Narrative Database
- [x] **Log Entries:** Automatically populated upon completing story beats in Story Mode.
- [x] **Structure:** Organized by Book -> Chapter -> Scene -> Beat.
- [ ] **Dynamic Summaries:** Instead of static text, summaries can expand to show "hidden lore" unlocked via high Intelligence stats.

### 2.2 Search & Discovery
- [x] **Filtering:** Filter by Book number or Chapter title.
- [ ] **Keyword Search:** Search the text of unlocked story beats.
- [ ] **Narrative Completion:** Visual indicator of % of total story beats unlocked in the current book.

---

## 3. Terminal 2: Relic Gallery (Collections & Artifacts)
A visual display for rare, lore-significant items that provide passive meta-bonuses.

### 3.1 Artifact Specifications
- [x] **Visual Representation:** 64x64 pixel art icons with a glowing rarity border.
- [ ] **Lore Inspection:** Clicking an artifact opens a dedicated modal with a rotating 3D-effect sprite and deep-lore text.
- [ ] **Passive Synergy:** Display active bonuses (e.g., "+5% Essence gain") and how they stack with other relics.

### 3.2 The "Rule of 4" Artifacts
1.  **Cracked Data Core:** Increases research speed in the upcoming "Academy" module.
2.  **Void Shard:** Reduces the "Focus" buff timer in the Battle Banner by 5 seconds.
3.  **Membrane Leaf:** Increases the effectiveness of training consumables.
4.  **Conduit's Focus:** Slightly increases critical click chance in Story Mode.

---

## 4. Terminal 3: The Hall of Echoes (Leaderboards)
Real-time competitive standings for the elite Drifters.

### 4.1 Competition Categories
- [x] **The Vanguard (Progression):** Rank based on furthest reached Scene.
- [x] **The Alchemist (Essence):** Rank based on total lifetime Elysium Essence accumulated.
- [ ] **The Swift (Speedruns):** Fastest completion times for specific boss scenes.
- [ ] **The Scholar (Lore):** Total number of Akashic Log entries unlocked.

### 4.2 Social Presence
- [ ] **Vessel Profile:** Clicking a leaderboard entry shows a snapshot of that player's character (Class, Level, Equipped Armor/Weapon).
- [ ] **Tiered Rewards:** Visual badges (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Cosmic) based on percentile rank.

---

## 5. Terminal 4: Achievement Matrix
A grid of 100+ challenges that track player milestones.

### 5.1 Achievement Types
- **Combat:** "Defeat 1,000 Sludges," "Deal 1M Damage in one session."
- **Narrative:** "Complete Book 1 without skipping audio," "Find all hidden beats in Chapter 2."
- **Economics:** "Accumulate 1M Gold in a single Story Mode run."
- **Idle:** "Reach Level 50 in all core skills."

### 5.2 Rewards
- **Shards:** Small amounts of premium currency for major milestones.
- **Titles:** Prefixes/Suffixes for the player's alias (e.g., "[Name] the Awakened").

---

## 6. Technical Implementation
- **Data Fetching:** Backend provides a consolidated `/api/game/home-base` payload to minimize requests.
- **Redux/Context Integration:** Artifact bonuses are calculated globally and applied to combat/training logic.
- **Persistence:** All "unlocked" states (Log, Relics, Achievements) are server-authoritative.
