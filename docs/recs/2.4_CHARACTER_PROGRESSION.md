# REC 2.4: Character & Progression Systems, Classes, and Skills

This document defines the character attributes, class system, and expansion logic for the Elysium Rising MMORPG.

---

## 1. Inspirations & Design Baseline
Character progression and scaling are modeled after the following titles. Use them as a baseline for stat weight and growth feel:

- **[Magic Research 2](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2864890/Magic_Research_2/):** Primary inspiration for the mix and matching of skills and resources, and the overall progression/growth scaling.
- **[Melvor Idle](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1267910/Melvor_Idle/):** Secondary reference for the depth and breadth of the skill system and the concept of "infinite" potential progress with enough time.

**Note:** These titles provide the foundation for scaling. The requirements listed below supercede them where they diverge (e.g., dual leveling system and IP-specific core stats).

---

## 2. Character Classes (Rule of 4)
Character classes are derived from the *Towers of Elysium* book trilogy. Each class features unique base skills and abilities.

| Class | Philosophy | Core Ability |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Engineer** | Defense & Construction | Energy Shields (Reduces damage). |
| **Conduit** | Offense & Cosmic Magic | Cosmic Pulse (AOE Burst). |
| **Drifter** | Speed & Invisibility | Memory Blur (Increases Crit Chance). |
| **Vessel** | Power & Divine Fury | Elder Fury (Temporary Damage Boost). |

## 2. Core Stats & Attributes
Derived from the book's power system:
- **Strength:** Increases click damage and physical resistance.
- **Agility:** Increases attack speed (auto-click) and critical hit chance.
- **Intelligence:** Increases skill power and reduces cooldowns.

---

## 3. Inventory System
- **Equipment Slots:** Weapon, Armor, Trinket.
- **Rarity Tiers:** Common (Grey) -> Uncommon (Green) -> Rare (Blue) -> Epic (Purple) -> Cosmic (Gold).
- **Lore Relation:** Items and equipment are derived from descriptions and artifacts found within the books.

---

## 4. Dual Leveling System
The progression system balances permanent character growth with narrative chapter progress.
- **Character XP (Permanent):** Earned via Idle Training and Story Mode completions. Increases permanent base stats and level benchmarks.
- **Chapter XP (Narrative):** Progression tracking through the Tower's narrative nodes. Unlocks new chapters and Story Mode scenes.

---

## 5. Skill System
- **Narrative Source:** Skills are based on the specific powers and abilities described in the books.
- **Active Skills:** 5 slots for in-session use in Story Mode.
- **Passive Skills:** Unlocked via Idle Training milestones.

---

## 6. Prestige (NG+)
Higher difficulty loops for completed chapters:
- **NG+ Tier:** Scaled enemies (higher HP/Damage multipliers).
- **Visual Swaps:** Palette swaps for enemies and environments to indicate NG+ level.
- **Exclusive Rewards:** Rare drops and prestige currency exclusive to higher tiers.

---

## 7. Expansion Instructions (Content Scaling)
To ensure the "Rule of 4" can be expanded as the story grows:
- **Adding a Class:** Insert a new row into the `character_classes` table and provide a sprite in `frontend/public/assets/classes/`.
- **Adding an Enemy:** Define `base_hp`, `base_gold`, and `sprite_key` in the `enemies` table.
- **Defining Skills:** Map skill names and effects to the JSON-based `benefits` field in the `skills` table.
- **Automated Extraction:** Tooling exists (Book Agent Reader) to identify new entities from the source text and generate seeding SQL.
