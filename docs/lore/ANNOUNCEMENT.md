## The Towers of Elysium Are Becoming a Game.

**Elysium Rising** is a browser-based incremental MMORPG set in the universe of the *Towers of Elysium* book trilogy.

Reality is a prison. The Eternal Engine hums beneath the surface of everything you know. And now, you can fight your way out.

---

### What Is It?

Elysium Rising merges **immersive audiobook narration** with **incremental RPG gameplay**. As you listen to the Towers of Elysium trilogy, the story literally powers your progression — every chapter unlocks new enemies, abilities, boss encounters, and lore.

- **Click** to generate Elysium Essence — the raw energy fueling your growth
- **Listen** to the audiobook as you play — each chapter unlocks new game content
- **Fight** chapter bosses born from the book's darkest moments
- **Compete** on global leaderboards and earn achievements

### The World of Etheris

Step into a constructed reality where cosmic horror meets Gnostic mythology. The entity known as Yaldabaoth feeds on divine sparks, the Eternal Engine recycles souls through endless loops, and a resistance of cosmic beings fights from beyond the prison walls.

Choose your class. Awaken your divine spark. Break the cycle.

### Four Paths to Awakening

| Class | Theme | Play Style |
|-------|-------|-----------|
| **Engineer** | Eternal Engine manipulation | Defense & construction — build shields from the substrate itself |
| **Conduit** | Cosmic energy channeling | Reality warping — bend the Akashic Flow to unmake enemies |
| **Drifter** | Memory-walking | Stealth & precision — slip between the cracks in Etheris |
| **Vessel** | Entity channeling | Divine fury — wield fragments of the Thirteen's power |

### Features

- **Narrative-Driven Progression** — Every book chapter maps to in-game levels, enemies, and story beats
- **Incremental Mechanics** — Power grows even while you're offline
- **Immersive Audio Integration** — The audiobook syncs with your gameplay
- **Competitive Leaderboards** — Race other players through chapters
- **Achievements & Lore Collections** — Unlock the deeper mysteries of Etheris
- **Free to Play** — No cost to start. Premium options are cosmetic only.

### Built From the Books

Every character, every enemy, every environment in the game is drawn directly from the trilogy. Stephen, Jamie, Xavier, Benji, Lady Illkeserod, Yaldabaoth — they're all here. The Akashic Realm, the Substrate, Elysium Station, the Grey — you'll explore them all.

If you've read the books, you'll recognize everything. If you haven't — this is your gateway in.
