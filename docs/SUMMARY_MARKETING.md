## 3. Key Marketing Themes

| Theme | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Constructed Reality** | Every setting is potentially a fabricated prison. Nothing is as it seems. |
| **Memory as Weapon** | Characters recover memories from past lives/timelines. Remembering is both salvation and torture. |
| **Cosmic Imprisonment** | The overarching plot is about being trapped in purpose-built realities (Etheris). |
| **Love as Cosmic Power** | Love transcends the cosmic prison systems — it's the one thing that can't be overwritten. |
| **Dark Humor** | The series blends existential horror with irreverent comedy. Depression, death, and cosmic dread are treated with dark wit. |
| **Technology as Catalyst** | The Eternal Engine, AI, and technological hubris drive the narrative. This is tech-horror, not magic-fantasy. |
| **Moral Ambiguity** | Heroes and villains are not clearly delineated. The protagonist helped build the prison. |

---

## 4. Genre Positioning

**What it IS:**
- Sci-fi cosmic horror with dark humor
- Psychological thriller with existential themes
- Technology-driven narrative (AI, constructed realities, simulation theory)
- Multi-timeline, multi-identity story

**What it is NOT:**
- Medieval fantasy
- Sword-and-sorcery
- Traditional MMORPG lore (no "ancient gods," "obsidian towers," or "prophecies")
- YA fiction (despite Book 2 featuring a teenage protagonist)

## 5. Visual Identity Notes (From Book Cover)

The primary book cover (`Books/NewCover04112025.jpg`) establishes the visual identity:

- **Dominant Color:** Deep crimson/red cosmic nebula swirl
- **Accent Color:** Cyan/teal glowing text and interface highlights
- **Background:** Dark cosmic void with nebula effects
- **Key Elements:**
  - Large translucent humanoid entity with glowing white eyes (cosmic being)
  - Enormous cosmic eye in upper-left corner (surveillance/omniscience)
  - A lone man in a business suit, seen from behind (the protagonist)
  - Glowing concentric rings on a grid/matrix floor (constructed reality)
  - Shadowy hooded figures in the background (The Pointers)
- **Aesthetic:** Cyberpunk meets cosmic horror. NOT medieval fantasy.
